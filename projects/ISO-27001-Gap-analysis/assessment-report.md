# Executive gap assessment

**Fictional Northbridge | Assessment 14 September 2026 | Baseline 31 August 2026 | v1.0**

## Conclusion and decision

Northbridge should prioritise identity, recoverability, data handling and response readiness before presenting itself as ready for certification. Of 93 applicable reference controls, only 5 have sufficient stated baseline support for an Implemented exercise rating; 61 are Partial and 27 Missing. This distribution describes the chosen scenario, not a measured real company's maturity.

Request the COO to assign and resource the P1 work within 30 days, approve accountable owners and review progress weekly. Drafted policy documents do not close 5.1 or related implementation gaps. The highest business concerns are unauthorised access to customer data, prolonged outage and insufficient visibility to contain compromise.

## Method and boundaries

The scope is the [company context](company-context.md). Map every Annex A reference to its local use, a synthetic observation, implementation status, action, owner, deadline and acceptance test. The [evidence catalogue](evidence-catalogue.md) makes the assumptions explicit. The mapping uses original topic shorthand rather than standard wording.

- **Implemented:** the stated scenario sample supports design and operation; limited-sample assurance only.
- **Partial:** a safeguard exists but scope, repeatability or supporting evidence is incomplete.
- **Missing:** no relevant control or evidence is described in the baseline. In an actual review this requires investigation, not a claim that no safeguard could exist.
- **Not applicable:** requires a documented risk-based reason. None selected here: Northbridge has people, an office, cloud dependencies and software development.

No averaging is used to declare compliance. Applicability is not the same as implementation. Each inclusion is justified by the business scope; the register is an input to a Statement of Applicability, not an approved final SoA. Security and the COO must validate necessity against assessed risks and obligations and record approval. Controls beyond Annex A may also be needed.

P1 means urgent exposure or a foundational dependency with a 30-day target (14 October 2026); P2 means important planned improvement within 90 days (13 December 2026); P3 means sustained assurance or longer-term improvement within 180 days (13 March 2027). These priorities are locally defined, not ISO-prescribed. Where operating evidence needs an elapsed observation period, the target covers configuration and initial testing only; retain a separately dated open assurance check. ACT-8.15 therefore cannot be fully verified closed after only 30 days. Where engineering delivery exceeds a target, implement an interim safeguard and escalate residual risk before the deadline.

## Full ISMS work remains

Annex A coverage alone does not establish conformity. Separately assess clauses 4–10: organisational context and interested parties; leadership and scope; risk assessment/treatment and objectives; resources and competence; controlled documentation; operational planning; monitoring and independent internal audit; management review; corrective action and improvement. Consider the 2024 climate-action amendment when reviewing context and interested-party requirements. This project does not claim those requirements have been audited.

## Limits and validation

All conclusions are conditional on the invented evidence. A real engagement should sample transactions, corroborate interviews, test settings, validate outsourced responsibilities and obtain independent assurance. Use the full licensed ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and relevant ISO/IEC 27002 guidance to validate the mapping before operational reliance.
