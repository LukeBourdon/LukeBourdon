**Northbridge | SIM-PHISH-2026-01 | Exercise closure: 31 August 2026 | Report prepared: 14 September 2026**

**All results are invented for this portfolio. No campaign was deployed, no staff were contacted and no actual credential submissions occurred.** The example's operational exercise is marked closed in the fictional timeline; outstanding programme improvements remain open. This does not establish that the proposed September programme is in operation.

## Executive finding

In the synthetic cohort, unique clickers fell from 24/120 (20.00%) to 10/120 (8.33%): a decrease of **11.67 percentage points**, or **58.33% relative** to baseline. Reporters increased from 30/120 (25.00%) to 60/120 (50.00%). Median reporting time did not improve (53.0 to 53.5 minutes among reporters); more reports do not establish faster response. These are descriptive calculations, not a causal estimate of training effectiveness. Same-cohort reuse, prior exposure, unscored lure difficulty and invented data prevent a real effectiveness claim.

## Illustrative intervention and resolution

In the fictional timeline, baseline debrief occurred on 4 August; all 24 baseline clickers completed private microlearning by 10 August, including the eight dummy submitters. A broader core refresher reached 114/120 by 21 August; six completions remained outstanding. Those training completion figures are scenario narrative assumptions, separate from the campaign event dataset.

Follow-up on 24 August records ten clickers and two dummy submissions. The exercise coordinator closes the sending/measurement window on 31 August after reconciling 120 records per round and preparing the report. Closure concerns the exercise window, not risk resolution or completion of every follow-up action. Click and report targets are met overall; the 100% learning completion target is not met (114/120 = 95%).
