Dates and status matter: baseline 31 August 2026; assessments and policy drafts 14 September 2026; future remediation is Open. The August phishing example is entirely synthetic and does not demonstrate operation of the proposed September programme.

Control references in the risk register map to ACT-[control ID] in the [gap action register](ISO-27001-Gap-analysis/action-register.md). R06 is also addressed by the payroll supplier decision. Training supports identity, data-sharing and reporting risks without replacing technical treatment.

All evidence and vendor responses are invented. No live testing, communications, procurement or technical implementation occurred. Documents and structured data are provided in Markdown and JSON for version control and review.
