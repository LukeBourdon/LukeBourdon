# Third-party security questionnaire

Complete for the exact contracted service, hosting locations and support arrangements. State Yes/Partial/No, describe scope and exceptions, and attach dated evidence. Marketing material alone is not assurance. Record respondent role/date and independent reviewer. These fields are pending for real use; the worked responses are explicitly fictional.

## Intake fields

Legal entity; service owner; service description; data types and volumes; hosting/access countries; integration privileges; business recovery needs; subcontractors; contract value/term; termination dependencies; respondent; assessment date; reviewer; next review. Avoid sending production secrets or personal records as questionnaire evidence; use redacted assurance artefacts.
