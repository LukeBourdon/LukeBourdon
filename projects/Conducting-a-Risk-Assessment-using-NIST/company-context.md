# Northbridge Cloud Services Ltd — case-study context

**Fictional portfolio exercise.** Northbridge, its staff, customers, vendors, evidence and results are invented. This is completed analytical project work, not a claim of employment, live testing, certification, implemented remediation or actual board approval.

Assessment date: **14 September 2026**. Baseline evidence cut-off: **31 August 2026**. Proposed remediation deadlines run from this assessment date. Policies are v1.0 drafts for board approval; they are not baseline controls already in force.

## Business and scope

Northbridge is a UK B2B workflow software company with 120 employees, a Cambridge office and hybrid working. It serves 60 SME customers and processes approximately 40,000 customer contact records. Annual revenue is assumed to be £8m. Departments: Engineering 35, Customer Operations 30, Sales 25, Corporate Services 20, Leadership 10.

Scope covers the hosted customer platform, application development, customer support, corporate identity and collaboration, HR/payroll, procurement and office operations. Technology consists of a UK-region cloud production platform, a separate development subscription, managed laptops, central identity, email, code repositories and a support SaaS. There is no owned data centre; cloud dependencies remain in scope through shared responsibility. Northbridge develops its own software and uses contract developers. No payment-card processing or patient health records are in scope. HR holds limited sickness and adjustment information, treated as Restricted.

## Accountability

The Board approves policy and risk appetite. The COO is the executive ISMS sponsor and owns operational continuity. The Security Lead coordinates assessments and incident response. The IT Manager owns identity and endpoints; Head of Engineering owns production and development; People Lead owns employment controls and learning; Privacy Lead owns data protection assessment; Procurement Lead owns supplier oversight; Facilities Lead owns office safeguards; Finance Director owns payment controls. These are fictional roles, not named real people.

## Business tolerances and baseline

Proposed business recovery objectives: customer platform RTO 8 hours / RPO 4 hours; corporate identity RTO 4 hours; support RTO 8 hours / RPO 24 hours. These are management targets requiring approval and validation, not measured capabilities.

The baseline has partial MFA coverage, quarterly patching, nightly backups without a recent full restore, ad hoc supplier checks, generic induction and incomplete offboarding. Specific invented observations appear in each assessment. Where evidence is absent, the assessment records uncertainty rather than assuming that a control operates.

## How the five projects connect

The gap analysis describes the baseline. The risk register prioritises exposure. The policy suite specifies the desired rules. Vendor assessment and training provide worked examples of operational GRC processes. Training results are synthetic historical exercise data, not evidence that the proposed new programme has been implemented. Project-level judgments are made against the same business context; a strong vendor does not imply Northbridge's own controls are strong.

## Evidence convention

Evidence references beginning SIM are explicit scenario assumptions. The quoted observations are sufficient for the desk exercise only. Production assurance would require access to source records, interviews, sampling and independent retesting. No sensitive real data is included.
