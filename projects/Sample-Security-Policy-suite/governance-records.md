## Exception record

Record: ID; request date; requester; policy and exact requirement; assets and users; business need; alternatives considered; likelihood and impact; current/residual risk; compensating controls; control owner; testing evidence; independent approver; decision; expiry (maximum 90 days); review date; closure or renewal evidence. Rejected and expired requests remain in the audit trail.

## Acknowledgement record

Record: person ID; role; policy ID and version; accessible delivery format; induction/refresh date; acknowledgement date; manager; absence follow-up; any unanswered questions. An acknowledgement is not proof that a technical control works.

## Incident record

Record: incident ID; reporter; discovery/awareness timestamps; affected assets/data; severity and rationale; commander; timeline in UTC; decisions and authorisers; containment/recovery evidence; chain-of-custody references; privacy risk assessment; notification decisions/times; communications; recovery sign-off; root cause; corrective action IDs; residual risk acceptance; operational closure authority/date.

## Access review record

Record: review period; system owner; account and employment status; each entitlement; business need; approve/remove/change decision; justification; reviewer and date; removal ticket; actual completion timestamp; independent revocation test. Retain according to the approved corporate records schedule with restricted access.
