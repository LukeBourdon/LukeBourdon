# Board decision paper — information security policy suite

**Northbridge Cloud Services Ltd | 14 September 2026 | Decision requested | Internal**

Sponsor: COO. Author role: Security Lead. Document: GOV-001 v1.0. Decision status: pending.

## Recommendation

Approve AUP-001, IR-001, AC-001 and DC-001 as the corporate minimum information security rules, approve the implementation plan below and require monthly assurance reporting to the COO with quarterly Board oversight. The proposed effective date is 30 calendar days after approval. Existing urgent incident reporting and containment must continue during transition.

## Why a decision is needed

The fictional baseline lacks approved topic policies. Incomplete offboarding, inconsistent MFA, unclassified exports and untested recovery expose customer information and service continuity. Informal instructions do not establish who can accept risk or what evidence management must retain. These policies assign those duties and make compliance testable.

## Scope and business implications

The suite applies to all employees, contractors and third parties using Northbridge information or systems, including remote work and cloud services. Suppliers must receive relevant obligations through contracts. The Board is being asked to approve rules; technical deployment still needs change control and testing. Restrictions on personal services and administrative rights may affect existing working practices. IT must provide approved alternatives before removing business-critical access.

## Proposed implementation and resource envelope

| Timing after approval | Deliverable | Accountable owner | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 0–10 | Publish controlled policies; brief managers; identify affected workflows | Security Lead | Version-controlled publication and manager briefing record |
| Days 0–30 | Classify priority data stores; issue approved transfer guidance | Privacy Lead | Owner decisions for customer, HR and finance stores |
| Days 0–30 | Obtain acknowledgements; provide accessible induction | People Lead | 100% active workforce completion or recorded absence follow-up |
| Days 0–30 | Enforce privileged MFA; disable legacy authentication after dependency checks | IT Manager | Configuration exports and negative authentication tests |
| Days 31–60 | Complete access reviews and resolve stale privileges | IT Manager | Owner-approved reviews and removal tickets |
| Days 31–90 | Run incident tabletop and recovery exercise | COO | Timed exercise, lessons and tracked actions |

Planning assumption: £12,000 one-off implementation support plus up to £1,500/month for incremental identity, monitoring and learning capability; 35 internal staff-days over 90 days. These are fictional planning estimates, not supplier quotations. Finance must validate procurement costs before commitment. They overlap with the gap-analysis programme and must not be added as a separate budget without de-duplication.

## Exceptions and assurance

No exception may waive a legal duty. The COO may approve a documented temporary policy exception for up to 90 days after Security and the asset owner assess exposure and compensating controls. High or Critical residual risk requires Board consideration before acceptance. Expired exceptions must be closed or explicitly renewed; silence is not approval. Quarterly reporting must show overdue exceptions, access removals, training completion, incident response performance and data handling findings.
