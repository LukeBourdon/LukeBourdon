# AUP-001 — Acceptable use policy

**Northbridge Cloud Services Ltd | Internal | v1.0 | 14 September 2026**

Owner: Security Lead. Approver: Board. Status: draft for approval. Effective: 30 days after approval. Review: annually from approval and following material change. Supersedes: first issue.

## Purpose and application

Protect Northbridge information and services while enabling legitimate work. This policy applies to all users of company accounts, devices, networks and information, wherever they work.

## Mandatory requirements

1. Use company accounts and approved services for business information. Do not forward work email to personal accounts or store company files in personal cloud storage. Limited personal use is permitted if lawful, low cost and does not interfere with work or security; company systems must not be used for harassment, unlawful content, unauthorised commercial activity or security testing without written scope approval.
2. Use managed, encrypted devices with endpoint protection and automatic updates. Do not disable safeguards, install unapproved software, connect unknown removable media or share accounts. IT authorises software and business-required peripherals. Personal devices must not store company information; any approved browser-only access must use IT's managed access conditions.
3. Use the approved password manager and MFA. Never share passwords, recovery codes or authentication approvals. Verify an unexpected MFA prompt through the service desk using a known contact route. Report repeated prompts immediately.
4. Follow [DC-001](data-classification-policy.md). Check recipients and sharing permissions before sending data. Use named recipients and expiry on external shares. Public links are prohibited for Confidential or Restricted data. Do not paste non-public information, source code or credentials into unapproved AI services. Approved AI use must follow the service's agreed data handling restrictions and human review of output.
5. Lock screens when unattended; managed devices lock after five minutes of inactivity. Keep paper and portable equipment secure. Use company remote-access controls, avoid shoulder-surfing and do not leave devices visible in vehicles. Public Wi-Fi must only be used with the approved secure connection; do not bypass warnings or install certificates to make access work.
6. Treat payment changes, urgent access requests and unusual file shares as verification events. Confirm material bank-detail changes through an independently known number and dual approval. Do not rely on the contact details in the requesting message.
7. Report suspected phishing using the reporting button or service desk. If a link was clicked or information disclosed, contact the service desk immediately, explain what happened and preserve the message. Do not delete evidence, investigate other users or attempt retaliation. Report lost devices as soon as discovered, with an internal target of 15 minutes.
8. Return devices, keys, tokens and records at departure or on request. Do not retain customer data or company material in personal storage. IT performs authorised wiping and disposal; users must not destroy records subject to a retention requirement or legal hold.

## Monitoring and privacy

Northbridge may record security-relevant authentication, device, network and service activity for defined security and operational purposes. Monitoring must be proportionate, documented in the staff privacy notice and accessible only to authorised personnel. The Privacy Lead reviews new monitoring arrangements before implementation. Routine monitoring is not permission for indiscriminate access to personal communications.

## Responsibilities and measures

Users protect and report; managers authorise business need; IT provides approved tools and enforcement; Security reviews incidents and exceptions. Monthly measures: acknowledgement coverage (target 100% of active users), overdue unapproved software findings (target zero beyond agreed deadlines), and time from loss discovery to report. The absence of reports is not evidence of compliance.


## Compliance, exceptions and review

Managers must make these requirements available and provide practical support. Personnel must complete induction and acknowledge this policy before unsupervised access, with annual refreshers. Suspected non-compliance must be reported to the Security Lead without delay. HR handles alleged misconduct fairly under the employment process; an accidental report is not itself grounds for punishment. Supplier failures are managed under contract.

Exceptions require the recorded business reason, affected assets, risk assessment, compensating safeguards, owner, expiry and approval described in the [board paper](board-paper.md). No individual may approve their own exception. The owner reports overdue actions monthly to the COO and reviews effectiveness annually. Version changes and approvals are retained in the controlled policy register.
